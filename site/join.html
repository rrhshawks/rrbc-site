Become a member

Membership is $10 a year or $50 for a lifetime. Students and staff join free. Open to parents, guardians, staff, alumni, and neighbors.

Open the membership form

What membership does

  • Pays for requests from teams, clubs, and classrooms.
  • Gives you a vote at monthly meetings.
  • Puts you on the list for volunteer calls and event news.

You do not need to be a member to volunteer or come to a meeting.
