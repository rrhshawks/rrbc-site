How to help

  • JoinMembership is $10 a year or $50 for a lifetime. Students and staff join free.
  • VolunteerWork a concession shift, help at a fundraiser, or pitch in from home.
  • DonateTax-deductible gifts from families and local businesses. EIN 91-1602695.
